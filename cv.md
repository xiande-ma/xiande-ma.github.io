# Xiande Ma · 马先德

**Ph.D. Candidate, Materials Science and Engineering**  
Northeastern University, Shenyang, China  
[maxiande@hotmail.com](mailto:maxiande@hotmail.com) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-0922-0811)

## Research profile

I investigate rare-earth magnesium alloys through experiments, atomic-resolution imaging, and computation. My doctoral research focuses on solute segregation, grain-boundary structure, interfacial mechanics, and plastic deformation in Mg–Nd and Mg–Sm alloys. I develop atomistic models and compare them with microscopy observations to explain how composition and defects affect material behavior.

## Education

- **Ph.D. candidate, Materials Science and Engineering**, Northeastern University, 2022–present.
- **M.Eng. and B.Eng., Materials Science and Engineering**, Shenyang University of Technology, 2015–2022.

## Research experience

**Rare-earth magnesium alloys and interface mechanics · 2022–present**  
Prepared Mg–Nd and Mg–Sm alloys and analyzed grain-boundary segregation and structural defects using HAADF-STEM. Used DFT and molecular dynamics to evaluate segregation energetics, interface stability, and shear response. Conducted high-throughput screening of magnesium-alloy tilt grain-boundary structures and processed simulation data with Python.

**Ageing and softening in dual-phase Mg–Li–Al(Zn) alloys · 2022–2024**  
Contributed materials-property calculations, microstructural analysis, and data organization for a project investigating over-ageing softening and its control.

## Technical skills

- **Simulation and HPC:** VASP, LAMMPS, OVITO; Slurm job scheduling; high-throughput structure screening.
